Artificial Intelligence in Australia

The World’s Readiness for Artificial Intelligence

This map shows the AI Preparedness Index (AIPI) across the world, with darker blue representing higher readiness for adopting and integrating AI, and lighter yellow/green representing lower levels of preparedness.

Australia is at the forefront of AI readiness, aligned with other advanced economies. In contrast, large parts of Africa, South America, and some Asian nations lag behind, highlighting a significant global divide in AI preparedness.

How Australian Students Are Using AI

Gender Share among AI Students Users

In Grade 9, female students make up the majority of AI users (58%), but their share steadily declines in higher grades. By Grade 10, males overtake females (54% vs. 46%), and this gap widens by Grade 12, where males lead at 56%. Overall, the chart highlights a shift from female dominance in earlier grades to male dominance in later years, suggesting changing patterns of AI engagement as students progress through school.
