Project Overview
Artificial intelligence is changing how people study, work and make decisions. This project examines Australia's position in this transformation through an interactive, data-driven visualisation.
The project connects three major questions:
How prepared is Australia for AI?
How are Australian industries and students adopting AI?
Will AI create new employment opportunities or contribute to job displacement?
The visualisation is designed particularly for students and early-career professionals who want to understand how AI may affect their education, skill development and career opportunities.
